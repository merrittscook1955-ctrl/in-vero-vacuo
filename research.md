# Research Overview

## Why this work matters
The classic Coleman–Callan partial‑wave method is powerful but limited. It relies on O(4) symmetry and breaks down in:

- multi‑field potentials  
- finite‑temperature backgrounds  
- curved spacetimes  
- gauge theories with nontrivial backgrounds  
- models without spherical symmetry  

My work develops a general lattice‑based method that remains valid in all of these regimes.

## Core idea
Compute fluctuation determinants directly on the lattice using:

- nonlinear SU(2) vertices  
- gauge–Goldstone–ghost Hessians  
- deflated stochastic‑Lanczos trace‑log  
- regulated Brillouin‑zone matching  
- continuum MS comparison  
- controlled spacing extrapolation
