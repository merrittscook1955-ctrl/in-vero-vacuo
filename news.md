# News & Updates

## October 2026

### Full SU(2) Nonlinear Vertex Module Validated
All scalar, gauge, Goldstone, and ghost vertices now pass momentum‑conservation
and Ward‑identity checks. This completes the nonlinear SU(2) vertex deck and
establishes a solid foundation for gauge‑sector matching and one‑loop analysis.

### First Regulated One‑Loop Sector Checks Completed
Initial one‑loop tests using a common IR regulator show:
- Higgs two‑point slope evaluated successfully  
- Scalar‑only four‑ and six‑point projections validated  
- Gauge‑sector prototypes behaving consistently under regulated comparisons  

These results confirm correct operator behavior across multiple sectors.

### L = 30 Determinant Run Completed
A major large‑lattice milestone:
- Bare logdet ratio: **−2057.27 ± 5.9**  
- Relaxation RMS: **3.27 × 10⁻¹²**  
- Full reproducibility archive generated  

These bare finite‑lattice results form the baseline for spacing extrapolation and
continuum comparison.

### Website Updated
Quantum‑simulation framing removed; project refocused entirely on lattice
determinant methods, semiclassical decay processes, and Higgs‑sector
metastability.
