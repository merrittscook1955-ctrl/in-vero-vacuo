In Vero Vacuo

Higgs‑Sector Vacuum Structure • Metastability • Lattice Methods

In Vero Vacuo is an independent theoretical research effort focused on Higgs‑sector vacuum structure, semiclassical decay processes, and nonperturbative lattice methods for fluctuation determinant evaluation. The project develops modern computational tools that extend beyond Coleman–Callan O(4) symmetry assumptions, enabling accurate decay‑rate prefactor calculations in multi‑field potentials, finite‑temperature backgrounds, curved spacetimes, and gauge theories with nontrivial structure.

Research Focus

  • SU(2) lattice machinery for semiclassical vacuum decay
  • Stochastic‑Lanczos trace‑log evaluation
  • Nonlinear vertices and fluctuation operator construction
  • Brillouin‑zone matching and MS comparison
  • Spacing extrapolation and continuum‑limit behavior
