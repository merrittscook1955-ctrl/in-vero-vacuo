In Vero Vacuo

Theoretical Research in Higgs‑Sector Vacuum Structure and Metastability

In Vero Vacuo LLC is an independent theoretical physics initiative focused on Higgs‑sector vacuum structure, metastability, semiclassical bubble dynamics, and nonperturbative lattice methods. This site provides access to research notes, methods, results, and formal documents.
