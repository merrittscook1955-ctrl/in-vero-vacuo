Nonperturbative Higgs Vacuum & Quantum Dark-Energy Modes

In Vero Vacuo LLC investigates the structure and decay of the Higgs-sector vacuum, combining lattice Euclidean methods with quantum simulation to explore ultra-light scalar modes connected to dark energy.

Explore the Research

Phase 1: Lattice Higgs Vacuum

O(4) bounce construction, fluctuation spectra, determinant ratios, and RG-improved Higgs potentials.

Phase 2: Quantum Spectra

Quantum Hamiltonian encoding, QPE/VQE via Qiskit, extended Higgs sectors and portal fields.

Dark-Energy Focus

Search for parametrically light modes and near-flat directions that can support dark-energy-like dynamics.
