# For Collaborators

## Overview
In Vero Vacuo welcomes collaboration with researchers working on vacuum
metastability, semiclassical decay processes, lattice methods, and gauge-theory
structure. The project provides a complete, validated computational framework
for fluctuation-determinant evaluation in scalar and SU(2) gauge theories.

If you are interested in applying these tools to your own research, joint
projects are possible across a wide range of physical settings.

---

## What I Provide
- **Validated SU(2) lattice determinant pipeline**  
  Full machinery for gauge, Goldstone, and ghost sectors, including nonlinear
  vertices, Hessians, and projector/routing infrastructure.

- **Scalar-sector determinant tools**  
  Complete scalar Hessians, nonlinear vertices, and MS comparison infrastructure.

- **Higgs Fluctuation Solver**  
  Exact Higgs/Goldstone operator construction with probe ensemble support.

- **Determinant Ratio Calculator**  
  SLQ-based trace-log ratio evaluation with deflation, alignment, and
  reproducibility logging.

- **Reproducibility archives**  
  SHA-256–verified bundles containing probe ensembles, relaxation logs, operator
  snapshots, and determinant-run metadata.

- **Technical documentation**  
  Detailed reports covering matching coefficients, regulated one-loop checks,
  large-lattice determinant runs, and continuum-limit behavior.

---

## What I’m Looking For
Collaborators interested in applying the machinery to:

- Finite-temperature tunneling  
- Multi-field potentials  
- Curved-spacetime backgrounds  
- Gauge theories with nontrivial structure  
- Early-universe phase transitions  
- Higgs-sector metastability studies  

Joint work is especially welcome in areas where traditional O(4) symmetry
assumptions fail and nonperturbative semiclassical tools are required.

---

## Collaboration Workflow
A typical collaboration proceeds through:

1. **Problem definition**  
   Identify the physical setting, background configuration, and required
   fluctuation operators.

2. **Operator construction**  
   Build scalar, gauge, Goldstone, and ghost Hessians using the validated
   machinery.

3. **Determinant evaluation**  
   Use SLQ trace-log methods, probe ensembles, and deflation to compute
   determinant ratios.

4. **Matching and comparison**  
   Perform lattice–MS finite-difference checks, spacing extrapolation, and
   continuum-limit validation.

5. **Joint reporting**  
   Prepare technical reports, reproducibility archives, and publication-ready
   documentation.
