About In Vero Vacuo

Mission

In Vero Vacuo is an independent theoretical research initiative focused on Higgs‑sector vacuum structure, metastability, and semiclassical decay processes. The project develops modern computational tools that extend beyond traditional Coleman–Callan O(4) symmetry assumptions, enabling accurate fluctuation‑determinant calculations in realistic physical settings.

Research Approach

The project combines semiclassical theory, lattice methods, and modern numerical techniques to evaluate decay‑rate prefactors with high precision. Key components include:

  • Nonlinear SU(2) gauge and Goldstone vertex construction
  • Exact scalar, gauge, Goldstone, and ghost Hessian operators
  • Stochastic‑Lanczos trace‑log determinant evaluation
  • Brillouin‑zone matching and MS‑scheme comparison
  • Continuum‑limit spacing extrapolation
  • Reproducibility‑verified probe ensembles

Background

Traditional methods work only in highly symmetric cases, leaving large gaps in our ability to study realistic vacuum‑decay scenarios. In Vero Vacuo addresses these gaps by building a complete determinant pipeline for both scalar and SU(2) gauge theories, validated through extensive cross‑checks, Ward identities, and regulated one‑loop comparisons.

Long‑Term Vision

The long‑term aim is to establish a comprehensive computational framework for vacuum‑decay studies across a wide range of physical environments:

  • Higgs‑sector metastability
  • Early‑universe phase transitions
  • Finite‑temperature tunneling
  • Curved‑spacetime backgrounds
  • Gauge theories with nontrivial structure
