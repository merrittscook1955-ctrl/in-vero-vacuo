Research Overview

Purpose of the Project

In Vero Vacuo investigates Higgs‑sector vacuum structure and metastability using modern semiclassical and lattice‑based computational methods. Traditional Coleman–Callan O(4) symmetry assumptions fail in many physically relevant scenarios. This project develops tools that remain valid when symmetry is broken, backgrounds are nontrivial, or multiple fields interact.

Our goal is to build a reliable, extensible framework for computing decay‑rate prefactors in settings where analytic methods are insufficient.

Why This Work Matters

Vacuum decay plays a central role in early‑universe cosmology, electroweak stability, and phase transitions in gauge theories. However, the fluctuation determinants that control decay‑rate prefactors are notoriously difficult to compute outside idealized symmetric cases.

  • Validated SU(2) lattice machinery
  • Stochastic‑Lanczos trace‑log evaluation
  • Nonlinear vertex construction
  • Brillouin‑zone matching
  • MS‑scheme cross‑checks
  • Spacing extrapolation

Current Directions

  • Multi‑Field Vacuum Decay

  • Gauge‑Theory Structure
  • Determinant Evaluation

Long‑Term Vision

The long‑term aim is to establish a robust computational framework for metastability studies across a wide range of physical settings.
