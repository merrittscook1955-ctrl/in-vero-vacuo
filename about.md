# About In Vero Vacuo

## Mission
In Vero Vacuo is an independent theoretical research initiative focused on
Higgs‑sector vacuum structure, metastability, and semiclassical decay processes.
The project develops modern computational tools that extend beyond traditional
Coleman–Callan O(4) symmetry assumptions, enabling accurate fluctuation‑determinant
calculations in realistic physical settings.

Our goal is to build a robust, extensible framework for studying vacuum decay in
multi‑field potentials, finite‑temperature backgrounds, curved spacetimes, and
gauge theories with nontrivial structure.

---

## Research Approach
The project combines semiclassical theory, lattice methods, and modern numerical
techniques to evaluate decay‑rate prefactors with high precision. Key components
include:

- Nonlinear SU(2) gauge and Goldstone vertex construction  
- Exact scalar, gauge, Goldstone, and ghost Hessian operators  
- Stochastic‑Lanczos trace‑log determinant evaluation  
- Brillouin‑zone matching and MS‑scheme comparison  
- Continuum‑limit spacing extrapolation  
- Reproducibility‑verified probe ensembles  

This machinery allows us to explore metastability in settings where analytic
methods fail or symmetry assumptions break down.

---

## Background
The project originated from the need for reliable computational tools capable of
handling fluctuation determinants in complex field configurations. Traditional
methods work only in highly symmetric cases, leaving large gaps in our ability
to study realistic vacuum‑decay scenarios.

In Vero Vacuo addresses these gaps by building a complete determinant pipeline
for both scalar and SU(2) gauge theories, validated through extensive
cross‑checks, Ward identities, and regulated one‑loop comparisons.

---

## Long‑Term Vision
The long‑term aim is to establish a comprehensive computational framework for
vacuum‑decay studies across a wide range of physical environments:

- Higgs‑sector metastability  
- Early‑universe phase transitions  
- Finite‑temperature tunneling  
- Curved‑spacetime backgrounds  
- Gauge theories with nontrivial structure  

The project seeks to make nonperturbative semiclassical calculations accessible
to researchers in particle physics, cosmology, and quantum field theory.
