# About

**Merritt S. Cook**  
Founder, In Vero Vacuo LLC  
Moore, South Carolina

I am an independent theoretical physicist developing numerical methods for quantum field theory. My work focuses on lattice approaches to fluctuation determinants, metastability, and gauge theories.

My current project is a full SU(2) lattice determinant pipeline designed to compute decay-rate prefactors in settings where O(4) symmetry reduction fails.
