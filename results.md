# Current Results

The scalar-only machinery is now fully validated and producing stable,
reproducible results across a range of backgrounds and lattice spacings.

## Large-Lattice Determinant Runs

### **L = 30, h = 0.8**
Recent large-lattice determinant runs demonstrate stable convergence and
reproducibility:

- Relaxation RMS: **3.27 × 10⁻¹²**
- Bare logdet ratio: **−2057.27 ± 5.9**
- 16 probes, 60 Lanczos steps
- 12 deflated modes per channel
- Full reproducibility record archived

These are bare finite-lattice quantities; continuum matching and spacing
extrapolation remain required for physical interpretation.
