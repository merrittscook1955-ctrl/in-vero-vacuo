Methods & Tools

Overview

The methods developed in In Vero Vacuo form a modern computational pipeline for evaluating fluctuation determinants in scalar and gauge theories. These tools extend beyond traditional O(4) symmetry assumptions and operate reliably in multi‑field potentials, finite‑temperature backgrounds, curved spacetimes, and gauge theories with nontrivial structure.

Lattice Determinant Pipeline

  • Nonlinear SU(2) vertex generation — constructs gauge, Goldstone, and ghost vertices with correct nonlinear structure.
  • Exact Hessian construction — builds scalar, gauge, Goldstone, and ghost fluctuation operators.
  • Stochastic‑Lanczos trace‑log evaluation — efficient determinant evaluation using deflated SLQ methods.
  • Bounce relaxation and probe ensembles — reproducible background and probe generation.
  • Routing and projector deck — consistent operator matching across gauge sectors.

Matching Infrastructure

  • Scalar and gauge vertices
  • Operator projectors
  • Regulated Brillouin‑zone integrals
  • Continuum MS amplitudes
  • Finite lattice–minus–MS differences

Validation Suite

  • Momentum conservation
  • Gauge‑invariance Ward identities
  • Ghost‑Jacobian consistency
  • Hessian agreement across sectors
  • Nonzero‑background FP matrix tests
  • Full routing/projector audit
