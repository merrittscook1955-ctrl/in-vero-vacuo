<h1>Modern Lattice Methods for Quantum Field Theory</h1>

<p>I develop numerical techniques for computing fluctuation determinants in scalar and gauge theories — enabling decay‑rate prefactors to be calculated in regimes where traditional analytic methods fail. My work focuses on validated SU(2) lattice machinery, nonlinear vertices, and stochastic‑Lanczos determinant evaluation.</p>

<p>Traditional O(4) methods break down in many physically relevant scenarios. This project builds tools for metastability studies in multi‑field potentials, finite‑temperature backgrounds, curved spacetimes, and gauge theories with nontrivial structure.</p>
